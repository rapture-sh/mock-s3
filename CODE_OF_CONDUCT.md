# Code of Conduct

## In short

Be decent. We are here to build a useful tool, not to score points.

## Expected

- Be respectful and patient, especially with newcomers and non-native speakers.
- Critique code, not people. "This diverges from S3 on empty prefixes" is
  feedback; "this is garbage" is not.
- Assume good intent, ask before escalating, and accept that maintainers may say
  no to a feature without that being a judgement of you.
- Respect the project's constraints even when you disagree with them. Argue for a
  change in an issue; don't smuggle it into a PR.

## Not acceptable

- Harassment, insults, or discriminatory language of any kind.
- Sexual language or imagery, unwelcome attention, or personal attacks.
- Publishing others' private information.
- Knowingly contributing behaviour that diverges from real S3 without saying so,
  or weakening a conformance test to make a change pass.

## Scope

This applies in the repository, issues, pull requests, discussions, and anywhere
someone is representing the project.

## Reporting

Report problems to the maintainers at [github.com/rapturedotsh](https://github.com/rapturedotsh)
by opening a private security advisory or contacting a maintainer directly.
Reports are handled discreetly. Maintainers may edit, remove or block, and will
explain when they do.
